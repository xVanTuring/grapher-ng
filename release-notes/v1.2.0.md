## Grapher NG 1.2.0

### 新功能
- **示例目录**：「文件 ▸ 打开示例」（以及欢迎窗口的「示例」）按功能分类列出 27 个现成示例——平面图的曲线与区域、参数滑块、切线与法线、数据与拟合；立体图的曲面与空间曲线、曲面交线、点线面、投影与切平面。每个示例在新窗口打开，拿来学习。
- **曲面交线**：选两个曲面（显式、参数、隐式任意组合），画出它们的交线；曲面隐藏后交线仍然保留。
- **点、投影与位置关系**：新增点图层（可带名称标注 A、B、C…）；投影图层把点、空间曲线或交线投到坐标面或任意平面上，支持斜投影和投影线；检查器里的「位置关系」面板选两个点 / 直线 / 平面，给出距离、夹角、垂足、交点、公垂线、交线，并可一键画出。
- **切线 / 法线与切平面**：平面图里给显函数、参数方程、极坐标画切线或法线；立体图里给曲面画切平面（半透明方片加法线），切平面可进位置关系面板、也能当投影目标。
- **半透明曲面**：曲面图层新增不透明度滑块。
- **选中联动**：在图层列表选中图层，画布里对应图形高亮；在画布里点击图形，选中对应图层。立体图里鼠标停在图形上，状态栏显示图层名和坐标。
- **文字标注与散点图**：平面图、立体图都能放自由文字；平面图新增散点图图层，粘贴 x, y 数据即可作图，可画最小二乘直线并显示均值、标准差、相关系数。
- **AI 助手**：新增 `relate`、`draw_relation` 工具，助手可以计算并画出点线面的位置关系；其余新图型也都能通过 MCP 使用。

### 改进
- 点的名称标注、文字标注会画进导出的 PNG。
- 多个来源（Debug 与发布）统一使用同一个签名证书。

## Grapher NG 1.2.0

### What's New
- **Example catalog**: File ▸ Open Example (and the Examples card in the welcome window) lists 27 ready-made graphs grouped by topic — in 2D: curves and regions, parameter sliders, tangents and normals, data and fitting; in 3D: surfaces and space curves, intersection curves, points/lines/planes, projections and tangent planes. Each opens in a new window to learn from.
- **Surface intersection curves**: pick two surfaces (explicit, parametric or implicit, any combination) and draw where they meet; the curve stays when the surfaces are hidden.
- **Points, projections and relations**: a new point layer (with name labels A, B, C…); a projection layer projects points, space curves or intersection curves onto a coordinate plane or any plane, including oblique projection and projecting lines; the Relations panel in the inspector takes two points / lines / planes and gives distance, angle, foot of the perpendicular, intersection, common perpendicular and line of intersection, and can draw them in one click.
- **Tangent / normal lines and tangent planes**: in 2D, a tangent or normal line for explicit, parametric and polar curves; in 3D, a tangent plane (translucent patch plus normal) for surfaces, usable in the Relations panel and as a projection target.
- **Translucent surfaces**: surface layers now have an opacity slider.
- **Selection linking**: select a layer in the list and its graph is highlighted; click a graph on the canvas to select its layer. In 3D, hovering shows the layer name and coordinates in the status bar.
- **Text labels and scatter plots**: free text in both 2D and 3D; a new scatter-plot layer in 2D takes pasted x, y data and can draw the least-squares line with mean, standard deviation and correlation.
- **AI assistants**: new `relate` and `draw_relation` tools compute and draw point/line/plane relations; every new layer kind is available through MCP.

### Improvements
- Point names and text labels are drawn into exported PNGs.
- Debug and release builds now share one signing certificate.
