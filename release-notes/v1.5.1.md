## Grapher NG 1.5.1

### 新功能
- **3D 轴向平面视图（正交投影）**：立体图可以沿 x、y、z 轴看，得到没有近大远小的 YZ、XZ、XY 平面视图。
  - 视图菜单里有「沿 X / Y / Z 轴看」和「正交投影」；在画布上按 `1` `2` `3` 切到三个视图，按 `P` 在透视和正交之间切换。
  - 切换时坐标盒和缩放不变，不用重新采样；切到平面视图后仍可拖动旋转。投影方式会存进文件，「重置视图」回到透视。
- **AI 助手**：`set_view` 新增 `view_along`（x / y / z）和 `projection`（perspective / orthographic）。

### 需要注意
- 分步讲解的相机暂时不记录投影方式，讲解里还不能切平面视图。

## Grapher NG 1.5.1

### New
- **Flat axis views for 3D (orthographic projection)**: a 3D graph can be viewed along the x, y or z axis, giving YZ, XZ and XY plane views with no perspective.
  - The View menu has "View Along X / Y / Z" and "Orthographic Projection". On the canvas, press `1` `2` `3` for the three views and `P` to switch between perspective and orthographic.
  - The axis box and zoom stay as they are and nothing is resampled; you can still drag to rotate from a flat view. The projection is saved in the file, and Reset View returns to perspective.
- **AI assistants**: `set_view` gains `view_along` (x / y / z) and `projection` (perspective / orthographic).

### Please note
- Lesson cameras do not record the projection yet, so a lesson cannot switch to a flat view.
