## Grapher NG 1.3.0

### 新功能
- **梯度可视化（立体图）**：新增「梯度场」图层，在显式曲面 z = f(x, y) 上画梯度箭头，沿曲面指向最陡上升方向，也可以把 ∇f 平放在地面；坡越陡箭头越长、越红，峰顶、谷底、鞍点没有箭头；可画下降方向 −∇f。
- **梯度流线**：从你列出的起点出发，沿梯度上坡（或下坡）画出路径，路径上有一个小球。起点的每一栏都可以写含参数的公式。
- **按坡度上色**：显式曲面可以勾选「按坡度上色」，变成 |∇f| 的热力图（平缓蓝、陡处红）。
- **参数播放**：参数面板里每个参数都有播放按钮，滑块自己来回走（或循环），用到它的所有图形实时变化；齿轮菜单设速度和方式。
- **让曲线逐渐画出来**：参数范围的端点可以写含参数的公式（如 `p*2*pi`），播放 `p` 曲线或参数曲面就一点点画出来。
- **位置也能是公式**：切线 / 法线 / 切平面的切点、文字标注的位置、投影平面的位置都可以写含参数的公式，播放参数切线沿曲线滑动、切平面绕曲面转。
- **示例目录扩充到 38 个**：新增「梯度」分类（山丘、向量场、鞍面、随滑块变化、攀向两座山顶、窄谷下降、热力图、起点绕圈）以及「自己画出来的曲线」「沿曲线滑动的切线」「绕抛物面转一圈的切平面」等。
- **AI 助手**：新增 `gradientField`、`gradientFlow` 图层，`color_by_slope`，`domain_formulas`，`at` / `offset` 可传公式等参数；每个新能力都有端到端的助手场景测试。

### 改进
- 平面图里点击曲线选中、选中的曲线有光晕；立体图里鼠标停在图形上显示图层与坐标。

## Grapher NG 1.3.0

### What's New
- **Gradient visualisation (3D)**: a new Gradient field layer draws gradient arrows on a surface z = f(x, y), along the steepest ascent, or ∇f flat on the floor; steeper means longer and redder, and there are none at peaks, pits and saddles; −∇f is available too.
- **Gradient flow lines**: paths that start at the points you list and follow the gradient uphill (or downhill), with a ball on each path. Every column of a starting point can be a formula with parameters.
- **Colour by slope**: tick it on an explicit surface to turn it into a heat map of |∇f| (blue where flat, red where steep).
- **Parameter playback**: every parameter has a play button; the slider sweeps by itself (back and forth or repeating) and everything that uses it updates live. The gear menu sets speed and mode.
- **Curves that draw themselves**: the ends of a parameter range can be formulas with parameters (such as `p*2*pi`); play `p` and the curve or parametric surface is drawn gradually.
- **Positions can be formulas too**: the point of tangency of tangents, normals and tangent planes, the position of text labels and of projection planes accept formulas with parameters; play the parameter and the tangent slides along the curve or the tangent plane travels round the surface.
- **38 examples**: a new Gradients group (hill, vector field, saddle, slider, two summits, narrow valley, heat map, moving start) plus "A curve that draws itself", "A tangent sliding along a curve", "A tangent plane circling a paraboloid" and more.
- **AI assistants**: new `gradientField` and `gradientFlow` layers, `color_by_slope`, `domain_formulas`, and formulas for `at` / `offset`; each new capability has an end-to-end assistant scenario test.

### Improvements
- In 2D, clicking a curve selects it and the selected curve glows; in 3D, hovering shows the layer name and coordinates.
